The barbers

NS Barbers is owned and run by the two barbers who cut there, Sergey and Nick.

Our goal at NS Barbers is to provide men with a complete grooming experience. When you look good, we feel good.

Sergey is a second-generation barber from Ukraine, trained through a family tradition. His mother was a barber for over 45 years and helped Sergey become a great barber. To this day people who receive great cuts from Sergey always come back.

His goal is to make sure that anybody who walks into the shop and sits in the chair will get the haircut they want, making sure the customer looks good when he walks out the door.

Nick Pagano is a third-generation barber and a graduate of Empire Beauty Academy in 2009. Both his grandfather and great uncle were barbers, and since graduating he has worked in upscale barber shops.

Nick’s goal when you take a seat in his chair is to provide you with professional services in an atmosphere where you can relax and enjoy the experience.

The thread

From growing up in shops to watching relatives perfect the craft, Nick and Sergey are hard workers who will continually exceed your expectations.
